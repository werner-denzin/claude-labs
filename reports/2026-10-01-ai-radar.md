### 1. Decision-only models arrive: TypeSafe's Jev gets cloned by OpenAI in a week

`AGENT TOOLING`

TypeSafe AI's Jev returns typed probabilities instead of text — Choice, Score and Noul answers with confidence values — at $0.042/M input tokens, 70-500ms latency, and reached 13% of Vercel AI Gateway's paid teams within 24h of its Oct 1 launch, double GPT-5.6's adoption rate. OpenAI cloned the idea as a "Decisions API" in about a week, using it to cut agent-monitoring cost from $372 to $2.94 per run. This is a new, cheap primitive for routing, classification and guardrail decisions inside agent loops.

### 6. GitHub Copilot's HydraFusion multi-model orchestrator reaches VS Code and the Copilot app

`IDE UPDATE`

HydraFusion treats model selection as an optimization problem across three strategies — Single, Cascade (cheap model drafts, strong model takes over on failed quality gates), and Critique (one model drafts, another reviews, the first revises) — and now runs inside VS Code 1.140+ and the Copilot desktop app, not just the CLI, with clearer progress indicators during long tasks. It is available to Copilot Pro, Pro+, Business and Enterprise users, with admin approval required for Business/Enterprise. This goes further than Copilot's single-model "Auto" picker by coordinating multiple models within one turn.

### 15. Adaptive-GEPA: one harness, many request types, no manual labels

`RESEARCH`
