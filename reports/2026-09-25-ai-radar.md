### 7. LangChain adds a CLI to fine-tune models from agent trajectories

`Tool use`

smithtune turns successful trajectories already logged in LangSmith into training data, runs supervised fine-tuning through Fireworks or Baseten (LoRA), and scores the tuned model against the base model via replay evaluation before deployment. LangChain's own numbers: a tuned Kimi K3 went from 90.0 to 96.0 on an internal agent benchmark, and a tuned Qwen-3.8-27B raised F1 from 48.9% to 53.7% while cutting model calls 29.8%. Useful specifically for repeated tasks where a team already has a track record of successful runs to learn from.

**Lens:** engineering
**Source:** [LangChain Blog](https://www.langchain.com/blog/langsmith-fine-tuning)
