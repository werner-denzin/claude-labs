### 10. Why fine-tuning jobs run longer than their FLOP estimate says

`Research`

A Hugging Face analysis of 272 SFT runs (7,382 GPU-hours on H200s) finds the FLOP-based time estimate most teams use explains little of the variance (R²=0.31, mean error 12.4h on jobs averaging 23h) — eval volume, checkpointing and I/O contention are the real drivers. A model using only pre-run config hits R²=0.98. No tool ships yet; logging eval frequency and checkpoint policy per job is the practical step today.

### 16. Cross-vendor code auditors catch 30% of planted bugs, with no plateau in sight

`Research`

Testing a setup where one vendor's model audits another's code and deterministic checks verify what's checkable, the authors measure real limits against ground truth: on general code with hidden tests, eight audit passes reach only 30.0% recall (CI 20.0-40.7%) at a 16.0% false-flag rate on correct code — with diminishing returns and no sign of a ceiling. A data point for how much to trust "AI reviews AI" as a safety net.

**Lens:** research
**Source:** [arXiv cs.SE (Software Engineering)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23250)
