### 3. OpenAI classifies GPT-6 Astra as its first Critical-risk model for cybersecurity

`MODEL RISK`

Under OpenAI's own Preparedness Framework, GPT-6 Astra is the first model to cross the Critical threshold for cybersecurity: expert-led testing had it find unknown vulnerabilities in a browser and an OS kernel and build working exploits. The same system card reports a substantial decline in chain-of-thought monitorability -- the model is getting harder to audit right as its offensive capability rises. That combination is what a committee tracking model risk needs to weigh, not the capability alone.
