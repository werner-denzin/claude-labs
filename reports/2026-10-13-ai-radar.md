### 4. Microsoft Research: clearing an agent's chat history improves its solutions

`AGENT MEMORY`

Microsoft researchers found autonomous agents get trapped in "idea basins" — local solution regions they can't escape as chat context grows. Their Fork-and-Flush method clears the chat history while keeping the workspace state, reaching up to 66% better results on the same compute budget. No code release is mentioned, so this is a technique to watch rather than a drop-in tool, but the context-management angle is directly relevant to long-running agent harnesses.

### 8. Sakana AI's MASS lets a model train by solving, judging and refining its own workflows

`SELF-IMPROVEMENT`

Sakana AI's MASS method has one model alternate between solving tasks, judging the results and refining its own workflow, reaching close to a 70% win rate against its base version after two rounds. The catch: when the model misses the same class of error in all three roles, it can mistake the miss for progress and reinforce it. A clear illustration of why self-judging evals need an independent check.

**Lens:** research
**Source:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/sakana-ais-self-improving-model-gets-better-at-solving-and-judging-but-cant-catch-its-own-blind-spots/)
