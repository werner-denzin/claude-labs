### 1. Two German courts now hold Google liable for AI Overviews' claims

`REGULATION`

The Aachen Regional Court applied the same reasoning a Munich court used after Google's appeal of that ruling quietly turned into a settlement: Google is liable when its AI Overviews state something false, in this case falsely attributing criminal offenses to a doctor. Two independent rulings now agree, and Google's attempt to overturn the precedent failed. Any product exposing an LLM's output as a direct claim about a person or company carries the same legal exposure.
