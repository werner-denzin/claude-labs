### 7. LangChain's model router cut coding-agent LLM cost 64% with no quality drop, in a 973-thread A/B test

`MODEL ROUTER`

LangChain built a model router into Open SWE's harness — not a generic gateway — using LangSmith traces to classify tasks and route them across three model tiers. In a 973-thread A/B test against always using the top-tier model, median LLM cost per thread fell from $2.61 to $0.94 (64% lower) with no significant change in merged-PR rate (29.2% vs 27.3%, p=0.49). A second test routing everything to the cheapest tier was pulled within a day over quality complaints, showing the tiering matters.

### 10. Ai2 cut GPU queue wait times 24x by replacing priority scheduling with time budgets

`SCHEDULING`

Ai2's infrastructure team runs GPU clusters for ~150 researchers at 2-3x demand over supply.
