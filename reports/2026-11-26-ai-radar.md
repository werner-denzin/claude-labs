### 18. How much reviewer effort catches plausible-but-wrong AI patches? Claude 5.5 models tested on SWE-bench

`RESEARCH`

Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 each reviewed the same 90 agent-written Django patches from SWE-bench Verified at low, medium and high reasoning effort. By a strengthened test suite (SWE-ABS), 55 of the 90 patches that passed the original benchmark tests were actually wrong — and the paper measures how much of that gap each effort level, and each model, actually catches.
