### 5. TypeSafe AI's Jev launches a new model category: classifiers instead of chat

`Model launch`

### 6. Meta's Muse had extensive agent security architecture — and a ClickFix 0-day anyway

`Security`

Muse isolates its agent in a sandboxed VM, routes credentials through a separate "Sentinel" approver, and never lets the model see passwords directly, assuming prompt injection will succeed and building OS-level controls around it. Researcher Patrick Wardle found a ClickFix-style flaw that lets local malware silently flip a hidden macOS setting, redirecting the user's dictated prompts to an attacker and letting them hijack the account with nothing to download. Even a defense-in-depth agent design had one setting that bypassed all of it.

**Lens:** engineering
**Source:** [Ars Technica AI](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/muse-metas-extraordinarily-privileged-ai-assistant-has-a-serious-0-day/) · also in [The Batch (Andrew Ng / DeepLearning.AI)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/how-to-secure-agents-for-the-masses)
