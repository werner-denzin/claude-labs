### 11. Message-passing between parallel agent threads beats routing through a coordinator

`Agent framework`

Carnegie Mellon researchers built Message Passing Language Models (MPLMs), where an LLM splits a problem across parallel threads that exchange information directly instead of funneling through a central coordinator thread that becomes a bottleneck. On 9x9 Sudoku, MPLM hit 100% success in ~15 seconds versus 93% in ~60 seconds for the coordinator-based approach; larger models showed roughly 2x latency improvements on general reasoning benchmarks. Works best when the communication pattern between threads is fixed in advance — a concrete design lesson for anyone building multi-agent orchestration today.

**Lens:** engineering
**Source:** [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/agents-work-better-when-they-can-pass-each-other-notes)
