### 1. Anthropic replaced git with an in-house system built for agents, not humans

`HARNESS`

Anthropic's merge volume grew from 2,500/day to over 30,000/day in four months as Claude agents and CI took over commits, so it built Scion: an always-online source control system with server-side history, content-addressed tiered fetching, and API access that needs no local checkout. Reported results: ~200x less CPU/memory than the old git-mirror fleet, checkouts down from minutes to seconds, CI gained ~25% capacity. Not released publicly, but the design ideas are being shared.

### 2. A behavioral harness got a cost-optimized model to match a frontier model's reliability

`HARNESS`

Naresh Jain had GPT-6 Sol (frontier) and GPT-6 Luna (cost-optimized) build the same API service with and without a behavioral harness. Sol passed every eval either way; unharnessed Luna succeeded in only 3 of 10 runs, harnessed Luna hit 10 of 10 — matching Sol's reliability at 33% less cost per run ($0.273 vs $0.406). A concrete data point for the build-vs-buy-bigger-model question every team here is already asking.

**Lens:** engineering
**Source:** [Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/articles/are-harnesses-worth-the-cost.html)
